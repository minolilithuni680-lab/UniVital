How Daily Habits Impact Your Brain & Sleep

Data insights powered by recent research from the Sleep Foundation.

Caffeine & Adenosine

Caffeine doesn't eliminate tiredness—it blocks adenosine receptors in your brain. Consuming coffee within 6 hours of bedtime reduces deep slow-wave sleep and disrupts nighttime clearance of neural waste.

  • Stop caffeine intake 6 hours before bed
  • Prevents afternoon fatigue crashes

Nutrition & Metabolism

Foods rich in tryptophan (oats, seeds, poultry) encourage melatonin synthesis. Avoid heavy, high-fat, or spicy meals 2 hours before bed to prevent elevated core body temperature and nocturnal acid reflux.

  • Opt for high-fiber, balanced evening meals
  • Enhances uninterrupted sleep continuity

Hydration & Vasopressin

During deep sleep, the brain releases vasopressin to retain water. Dehydration during sleep causes muscle cramps and morning headaches. Taper fluid intake 2 hours before bed to minimize awakenings.

  • Drink 2 Liters of water evenly throughout the day
  • Saves your bladder for restful sleep
