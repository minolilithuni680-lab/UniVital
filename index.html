How UniVital Calculates Your Health Suggestions

Our diagnostic engine analyzes 5 core vital domains to generate custom, student-focused optimization tips.

1. Hydration & Electrolytes

Dehydration reduces total blood plasma volume, lowering oxygen transport to brain cells and impairing concentration. We calculate fluid intake distribution to optimize cellular hydration without disrupting sleep.

2. Caffeine & Stimulants

Caffeine blocks adenosine receptors in your brain to mask fatigue. We measure timing cutoffs to prevent coffee consumed late in the day from destroying deep, slow-wave sleep.

3. Sleep & Rest Quality

During REM and slow-wave sleep, your brain consolidates memory and clears neurotoxic waste. Tracking sleep cycles allows us to recommend optimal bedtime windows for exam recall.

4. Diet & Nutrition Tracker

Nutrient timing dictates blood glucose stability. Balanced protein and tryptophan-rich meals encourage natural melatonin synthesis, preventing late-night energy spikes and brain fog.

5. Activity & Fitness

Regular aerobic activity stimulates Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF) for neuroplasticity and flushes excess cortisol out of your nervous system to ease exam stress.
